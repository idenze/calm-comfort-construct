A record of Igbo and African history

The histories of towns, clans and kingdoms, kept where they can be cited.

Ozikoro gathers town and clan histories, oral testimony, archive documents and the published work of African researchers — each entry carrying its period, its place and its sources.

Search and browsing work without JavaScript. Every result page has a stable address you can cite.

The institution

Ozi Ikoro Limited

The company that publishes Ozikoro, Ozituma and the Academy. One owner, one standard of sourcing, one address a researcher or a partner can rely on.

What the platform is for

To hold the histories of Igbo and African towns, clans and kingdoms in a form that can be searched, read at length, and cited — and to make the work of African researchers findable by the people who need it.

The platform is being built in layers. The dictionary and the Academy are running. The history archive and the researchers network are the work in front of us. Geography and mapping, genealogy, and diaspora reconnection are planned and not yet built; where this site shows them, it shows them as planned.

The standing rules

These are not an editorial preference. They are the terms on which the material is held, and they are published here so that anyone entrusting something can see them before they decide.

  • Histories are told as the record supports them. No people, clan or town is described as having a non-Igbo origin, and no account is written from the coloniser's vantage point.
  • Nothing is invented. No fabricated figures, no stock photograph standing in for a real town, person or document, no gap quietly filled.
  • A gap is published as a gap. An entry that lacks a source says so on the entry itself.
  • The depositor sets the terms. Material entrusted to the archive remains the property of the community, family or institution that holds it.

Entrusting a history or a collection

For a town union, a clan council or a family deciding whether to place material here, the questions that matter are who will see it, what we may do with it, and whether it can be withdrawn. The answers are settled in writing before anything is accessioned.

You choose, object by object, whether it is open, available by request, or restricted to the institutional tier. You may place a condition on reproduction. Culturally sensitive material is not published in full, and a request to withdraw is honoured. Where consent covers a transcript but not a recording, the record says so and the recording stays closed.

Access tiers

Open material is readable by anyone, with no account. Material held by request is described in full and released by the holder. The institutional tier is for verified researchers and partner institutions, and exists because some of the record is sensitive and open publication would be a breach of the terms it was given under — not to put a price on access.

Citing the platform

Every entry, object and publication has a permanent address and a dated version. Revisions are added to the record; addresses do not change. The three sites are cited as one institution, published by Ozi Ikoro Limited.

Privacy

Reading the archive requires no account and sets no tracking. Accounts exist for researchers and for contributors, and hold what they are given: a name, an institution, an email, and the work published. Living people named in deposited material are handled under the deposit's own terms, and genealogical material — when that layer is built — will carry its own privacy controls for living relatives.

To be supplied: the full privacy notice and the data controller's registered particulars.

Licensing and reuse

Each object states its licence on its own record. Where material is openly licensed, attribution is required and the archive reference should be given. Where it is not, the holder's permission is required and the route to ask is on the object.

Why it matters

Oral tradition is a source, not a rumour

Recorded as told

Each tale keeps the words, the teller and the place it was told. We do not rewrite oral tradition to sound like a textbook.

Cited like any source

A folktale on Ozikoro carries the same permanent address and ready-made citation as an academic paper.

Open to every family

If a story lives in your family or your town, you can add it — with the teller named and the rights kept by the community.
